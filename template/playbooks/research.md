# Playbook: research — questions, comparisons, due diligence

## Always

- Catalogue dimensions: **source quality**, **decision clarity**.
- Fixed seats: domain expert, methodologist, decision-maker, skeptic (+1–2 from triage).
- Prototype means: an annotated source map — every source found, graded, dated, and what it would contribute to the answer.
- The answer must name the decision it enables and the confidence it carries.

## Stage 0

See `playbooks/_meta.md` Stage 0. Research-specific: write the question as one sentence B could say yes/no or pick-one to; name the decision it feeds; kill criteria include "question is unanswerable with public sources" and "answer would not change the decision".

## Stage 1

Validate. Is the question answerable, and worth answering? Scan for existing answers (reviews, meta-analyses, reputable comparisons) — if a sufficient one exists, the recommendation is to adopt it, not redo it. `research.md` cap 120 lines. Panel, score, `gates/01.md` (hard).

## Stage 2

Plan → `plan.md`: method (sources, inclusion criteria, how conflicts between sources are resolved), output format, budget, human gates (paid databases, expert contact).

## Stage 3

Prototype → `prototype/source-map.md`: table of sources (title, date, type, independence, grade A–D, what it answers). Re-score on source quality. Steer.

## Stage 4

Build → `build/answer.md`: the answer first, confidence, the evidence by sub-question, disagreements between sources and how resolved, what would change the answer. Cap 200 lines plus appendix.

## Stage 5

Review. Panel; methodologist seat must specifically attack independence of sources. Final score. `gates/05.md` (accept). Export.
