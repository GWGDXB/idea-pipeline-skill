# Playbook: create — creative platforms, formats, content, campaigns

## Always

- Catalogue dimensions: **originality**, **audience fit**.
- Fixed seats: audience, critic, producer, rights/IP, skeptic (+1–2 from triage).
- Prototype means: a sample of the thing — one episode, one piece, one format run-through — good enough to show a stranger.
- Rights: any use of third-party material, names, likenesses or brands is flagged by the rights/IP seat and is a human gate before publication.

## Stage 0

See `playbooks/_meta.md` Stage 0. Create-specific: name the audience precisely (who, where they already spend attention), the format, and the distribution channel. Kill criteria include "audience cannot be reached without paid acquisition" and "format exists and is well served".

## Stage 1

Validate. Audience evidence (size, where, what they consume now — ≥3 sources), comparable formats and their performance with dates, platform rules that constrain the format. `research.md`. Panel, score, `gates/01.md` (hard).

## Stage 2

Plan → `plan.md`: definition of done (what exists, where it is published or ready to publish), the riskiest creative assumption, production method and tools from `tools/current.md`, human gates (publishing, paid tools, collaborators).

## Stage 3

Prototype → `prototype/` with the sample and `prototype/RESULT.md` on what it demonstrates and what it doesn't. Re-score on originality and audience fit. Steer.

## Stage 4

Build → `build/`: the full first set (e.g. first 5 pieces, the format bible, the production checklist). Gate before publication or any external contact.

## Stage 5

Review. Panel reviews the actual pieces. Final score. `gates/05.md` (accept). Export.
