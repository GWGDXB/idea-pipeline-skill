# How a tool or model earns a swap

A proposal in `pending-swaps.md` must show all of these, each with a dated source:

1. **Better on the step's own measure** — for models: quality on a held-out sample of this pipeline's outputs (one past idea re-run, scores compared); for tools: the step's acceptance check passes faster/cheaper.
2. **Cheaper or equal** per iteration at the quality bar, or the quality gain is named and B wants it.
3. **No new dependency** B must pay for or sign up to, or the cost is in the proposal.
4. **Portable** — works on the Mac and on a Linux VM; no app-settings state.
5. **Reversible** — the swap can be undone by editing `tools/current.md` only.
6. **Stable for ≥ 2 weeks** since release (no day-one swaps).

Swaps apply to new ideas only. Running ideas finish on the tools they started with.
